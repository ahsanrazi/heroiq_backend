How HeroIQ Search Works

Two specialised workers run side by side — one answers searches instantly, the other prepares your content in the background — so searches stay fast even while thousands of pages are being indexed.
Worker 1

Answering a search

⚡ about half a second
Visitor asks
“Do you offer root canals?”
→
Search Worker
Finds the best matching pages
→
Instant answer
Ranked results in the widget

Uses the AI understanding and Search Index that Worker 2 has already prepared — so there’s nothing heavy to do at search time.

Worker 2

Preparing your content

🌙 runs in the background
Your website pages
Posts, pages, services…
→
Job Queue
Pages line up, waiting their turn
→
Indexing Worker
Reads & understands each page
→
Ready to search
Saved to the Search Index

This worker does all the heavy lifting separately, so indexing a large site never slows down live searches.

Why it’s built this way

✓

Searches stay fast — even when lots of people search at once.

✓

Big sites index smoothly — thousands of pages with no downtime.

✓

✓
